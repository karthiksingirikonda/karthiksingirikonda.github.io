Privacy policy

Last updated: Sun 1/11/26

This policy explains what the Kartha app collects, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you can delete your account. It is written in plain English. If anything is unclear, write to karthik.ca.1@gmail.com.

The short version. Kartha is a work chat. Your company's Owner and Manager can view work chats, and they are kept as company records. Kartha has no ads, does not sell data, and has no tracking.

1. Who runs Kartha

Kartha is made and run by Karthik Singirikonda, Hyderabad, Telangana, India (called "we" or "the app owner" in this policy).
Email: karthik.ca.1@gmail.com

Kartha is used by companies (for example interior and construction firms) for their staff, supervisors and vendors. The company that invited you is called "your company" below.

  • Work records (chats, files, questions, figures) belong to your company. Your company decides how they are used. We keep and process them for your company.
  • Your sign-in account (who you are, which phone you use) is looked after by us.

2. What Kartha collects

From Google sign-in

  • Your name, email address and profile photo. Kartha has no passwords; you sign in with your Google account.

What you or your company add

  • Your phone number and role (for example Supervisor or Vendor), when your company adds them.
  • Messages, voice notes, photos, files (such as quotations, purchase orders, bills, payment proofs), questions and answers, private notes, decisions and rates.
  • Call transcripts that your company adds to a chat.
  • Amounts typed on work posts (for example PO value, amount paid, expenses).
  • Problem reports you send from Help, with any screenshot you attach.
  • Items your company forwards to its Kartha email address (for example a message shared from another chat app).

Collected by the app

  • A device id: a random number made by the app on your phone. It is used for the one-phone rule (one account works on one phone at a time). It is not your phone's IMEI or serial number.
  • A notification address for your phone, if you allow notifications.
  • Your settings: language, notifications on or off, and similar choices.
  • "Seen" times: when you last opened a chat, so the person who asked a question can see "seen 10:42".
  • Usage counts: numbers only, such as messages sent, files saved, minutes of voice transcribed, and calls made by the app each day. These counts never contain what you wrote.

Kept on your phone

  • A copy of recent screens and your last opened chats, so Kartha opens quickly and can be read without signal, and messages waiting to be sent when there is no network. This copy is cleared when you sign out.

Kartha does not read your contacts, SMS, call log or location.

3. How it is used

  • To run the chat: show messages to the right people, save files, keep questions and answers.
  • To sign you in and keep one account on one phone.
  • To send phone notifications and short alert mails about new messages.
  • To translate messages, turn voice notes into text, and read messages aloud, when your company has these features on.
  • To make a daily chat record for your company, and an export of its data when your company asks.
  • To fix problems you report, and to count usage so your company's plan and costs are correct.

5. Who can see it

Your company can view work chats. Your company's Owner and Manager can view work chats and project groups, and keep them as company records. The company's Owner can also export all of the company's chats and files. Please use Kartha for work, not for personal matters.
  • People in the same chat see that chat's messages, files and questions.
  • Project sections (for example BOQ or PO) are seen only by the people your company picks for that section.
  • Private notes are seen only by the writer and the person tagged.
  • Vendors see only their own work and pricing chats and their own figures, never another vendor's.
  • Your company's Google Drive: people your company gives access to its Drive can see the files and chat records saved there.
  • The app owner can see people, roles, devices, usage counts and problem reports, to run and support Kartha. Where a company has asked for it, the app owner can also open that company's chats read-only to support its work (for Design Interio, Karthik Singirikonda is also the company's Consultant Manager). Private notes between other people stay hidden even then.
  • Phone notifications show only chat names and counts, never message text.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it (for example a valid order from a court or government authority in India).

6. Google services used

  • Google Sign-In: to sign you in.
  • Google Drive: to store files and chat records in your company's Drive.
  • Google Sheets: to store messages and app records.
  • Gmail: to send short alert mails and to receive items your company forwards to its Kartha address.
  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text: to turn voice notes into text.
  • Google Cloud Translation: to translate messages into each reader's language.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: to read messages aloud.
  • Google Cloud text summaries: to make short summaries of pricing and work chats, and to list changes found in call transcripts, when your company uses these features. The written summary is not kept by the app owner.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: to send phone notifications.

Audio and text may be processed outside India. When voice, translation, read-aloud or summary features are used, the voice note or text is sent to Google for that task and may be processed in Google data centres outside India (for example in the United States). Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

These features are switched on per company. If they are off, nothing is sent for them.

7. The company's accounts link (read-only)

If your company uses an accounting app (for example Zoho Books), it can link it to Kartha so vendor bills, payments and dues show in projects. This link is read-only: Kartha only reads. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in the company's accounts. A small copy of bills and payments is kept with the company's records so Kartha does not have to ask the accounting app every time.

8. What Kartha does not do

  • No ads.
  • No selling or renting of your data, ever.
  • No tracking, no analytics tools, and no outside scripts on this website.
  • No use of your messages for marketing.
  • No messages sent in your name without you pressing Send (Kartha saves mail drafts; you send them yourself).

9. How long it is kept

  • Work records are kept while your company uses Kartha, because they are the company's records. Your company can delete them.
  • When a company stops using Kartha, it gets an export of its data, and its data is then deleted from the app owner's account.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days and then removed.
  • Sign-in and usage logs are kept for up to 1 year, for security and billing.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in, devices and personal details are deleted within 30 days (see section 10).

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your Kartha account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my account.
  • By email: write to karthik.ca.1@gmail.com from your registered email, with the subject "Delete my Kartha account".

Your sign-in, devices and personal details are deleted. Work messages and files you sent stay with your company as its records, until the company deletes them. Full details: Delete account.

11. Not for under-18s

Kartha is a work app for adults. It is not for anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

12. Security

  • Sign-in is only through Google; Kartha keeps no passwords.
  • One account works on one phone at a time. Signing in on a new phone signs the old one out.
  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Each company's data is kept separately. People see only the chats and sections they are allowed to see.
  • Keys for outside services are kept in protected script settings, never in the code or in sheets.
  • Your company can remove a person at once, and their phone loses access.

No system is perfectly safe. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell your company and, where needed, you and the authorities, as the law requires.

13. Your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what personal data is held about you and how it is used;
  • ask for wrong or old data to be corrected or completed;
  • ask for your personal data to be deleted (see section 10);
  • withdraw your consent (this means you stop using Kartha; work records stay with your company);
  • name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act;
  • raise a complaint, and get an answer.

For work records, your company decides how they are used; write to your company, or to us and we will pass it on and help. For your sign-in account, write to us. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. For big changes, your company will be told, and a note will be shown in the app.
