Delete your Kartha account

Kartha — team chat for site work, by Karthik Singirikonda

Way 1: in the app

  1. Open Kartha from the KARTHA icon on your phone.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings (bottom bar).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Way 2: by email

Send an email from the email address you use for Kartha:

  • To: karthik.ca.1@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my Kartha account
  • Optional: your name and company, so we find you faster.
Write the email

We only act on mails from the registered email address, so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: you can no longer open Kartha with this account.
  • Your devices: the phone's device id and notification address.
  • Your personal details: phone number, profile photo, language and other settings.

What is kept, and why

  • Work records (messages, voice notes, photos, files, questions, answers, rates and figures you sent in work chats) belong to the company you worked with. They are its business records, and other people in those chats rely on them. They stay with the company until the company deletes them. Your name stays next to the messages you sent, so the records still make sense.
  • Backups: any copy of your personal details in backups is removed within 30 days.

If you want something removed from a company's work records, ask that company. We will help pass the request on.

How long it takes

Your account is deleted within 30 days. When it is done, we send a short confirmation to your email.
